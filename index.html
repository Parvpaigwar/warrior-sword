A full-metal decorative sword — mirror-polished stainless blade, antique-copper dragon hilt with a gold coil grip — supplied with a 104 cm dark walnut wall plaque. Arrives ready to hang.

What's in the box
  • Display sword, approx. 95 cm (blunt edge)
  • Dark walnut wall plaque, approx. 104 cm
  • 2 brass pegs + metal tip cup (fitted on plaque)
  • Screws & wall plugs for the 2 keyhole slots on the back
Specifications
  • Blade: stainless steel, 3–4 mm, mirror polish, black etching both sides
  • Pommel & guard: die-cast metal, antique copper / bronze finish
  • Grip: dark sleeve with gold-tone twisted wire spiral
  • Guard eyes: amber / gold cabochons
  • Plaque: walnut-finish wood, satin lacquer, white tribal print
Care

Wipe the blade with a soft dry cloth; a drop of light machine oil now and then keeps the mirror finish bright. Avoid damp walls and direct moisture. Dust the plaque with a dry cloth.

Questions

Before you claim it.

Anything else? Message us and we'll help you out.

Is the sword sharp?

No. It is a decorative display piece — the edges are blunt and unsharpened and the tip is slightly rounded. It is not a functional weapon.

Is it real metal?

Yes. The blade is stainless steel with a mirror polish, and the dragon pommel and guard are die-cast metal with an antique copper finish.

How do I hang it?

The plaque has two keyhole slots on the back. Screws and wall plugs are included — fix two screws to the wall, slide the plaque on, then rest the sword on the brass pegs with the tip in the cup.

How much wall space does it need?

Plan for about 110 cm of height and 25 cm of width. The plaque itself is roughly 104 cm tall and 23 cm wide at the top.

Can I take the sword off the plaque?

Yes — the sword simply rests on the pegs and tip cup, so you can lift it off to dust it or display it on its own.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes — pan-India delivery is available. We deliver across India; once your order is confirmed we'll share dispatch and tracking details with you.
